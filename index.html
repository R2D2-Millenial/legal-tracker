National Biodiversity Authority Reports Collection of ₹9.68 Crore Under ABS Framework

Shift in Legal Position

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) reported a collection of ₹9.68 crore under the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework, indicating sustained regulatory enforcement. Under the Biological Diversity Act, commercial entities utilizing Indian biological resources must share a percentage of their revenue or enter into ABS agreements, contrasting with previous lax compliance periods.

In-House Counsel Action Items
  • Audit the R&D and manufacturing supply chains to identify any Indian biological resources, raw materials, or traditional knowledge being utilized.
  • Ensure corresponding approvals are active and ABS agreements are duly executed with the NBA or respective State Biodiversity Boards (SBBs).
  • Reconcile calculated ABS dues against actual payments to prevent sudden enforcement actions, intellectual property registration blocks, or financial penalties.
