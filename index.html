National Biodiversity Authority Collects ₹9.68 Crore Under Access and Benefit Sharing Framework

Regulatory Shift

The National Biodiversity Authority's collection of ₹9.68 crore highlights rigorous monitoring of Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) compliance. Commercial entities utilizing Indian biological resources or associated traditional knowledge must strictly adhere to sharing benefits with local communities, contrasting with earlier periods of lax enforcement and pending amendments.

Counsel Action Items
  • Identify and audit all raw materials and biological resources sourced within India for R&D or commercial production.
  • Ensure necessary approvals or intimations are filed with the State Biodiversity Boards (SBB) or the NBA.
  • Re-verify the computation of ABS liability (typically ranging from 0.1% to 0.5% of the annual ex-factory sales) to avoid retrospective penalties and litigation.
