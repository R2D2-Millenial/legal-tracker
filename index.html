National Biodiversity Authority steps up enforcement of Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) compliance

Shift in Legal Position:

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) has collected INR 9.68 crore under the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework during April-September. This underscores intensified regulatory scrutiny and compliance enforcement on businesses utilizing Indian biological resources. Previously, enforcement of ABS compliance was relatively passive, but active collection trends signify strict audit protocols on commercial utilization and patent filings.

Legal & Compliance Action Items:

  • • Conduct an internal compliance audit of all bio-resources, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and natural raw materials sourced within India to ensure proper NBA or State Biodiversity Board (SBB) approvals are in place.
  • • Verify that all patent applications utilizing Indian biological resources have obtained the mandatory prior clearance from the NBA under Section 6 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.
  • • Review and align commercial agreements with local suppliers to clearly demarcate ABS payment liabilities and ensure indemnity against non-compliance by intermediaries.
