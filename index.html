National Biodiversity Authority Secures INR 9.68 Crore Under Access and Benefit Sharing Framework

Regulatory Shift

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) reported a collection of INR 9.68 Crore under the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework during the April-September period. This underscores a significant push toward stricter compliance monitoring and audit of corporate entities utilizing Indian biological resources, shifting away from historical leniency.

Counsel Action Items
  • Conduct an internal audit of raw material sourcing to verify if any biological resources or traditional knowledge of Indian origin are utilized in manufacturing or R&D.
  • Ensure all required approvals (Form I to Form IV) are active and filed with the NBA or State Biodiversity Boards for commercial exploitation and IP filings.
  • Validate that ABS fee calculations align strictly with the Biological Diversity Regulations to avoid potential prosecution or operational blockages.
