National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) Enforces Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Collections

Shift in Legal Position

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) recorded collections of ₹9.68 Crore under the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework, indicating a strict supervisory posture. This highlights the ongoing enforcement of the Biological Diversity Act, which mandates that companies commercializing Indian biological resources or associated traditional knowledge share financial benefits with local communities, contrasting with historic lapses in compliance tracking.

Legal & In-House Action Items
  • Identify and audit all raw materials, R&D projects, and commercialized formulations to detect usage of Indian biological resources.
  • Verify that necessary approvals from the State Biodiversity Boards (SBB) or the NBA (Form I to IV) are in place for all domestic and export products.
  • Compute applicable ABS liability (generally ranging between 0.1% to 0.5% of the ex-factory sales of the product) to prevent retrospective penal action and compounding fees.
