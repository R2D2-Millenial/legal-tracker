National Biodiversity Authority Enforces Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Compliance for Bio-Resources

Shift in Legal Position:

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) has intensified enforcement of the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework under the Biological Diversity Act, securing INR 9.68 crore in ABS collections over a six-month period. Under the recently amended Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, compliance protocols and benefit-sharing obligations for companies commercializing Indian biological resources are subject to strict regulatory scrutiny, making voluntary declarations and structured agreements essential to avoid legal actions.

Legal & In-House Action Items:

  • • Conduct a comprehensive compliance audit of all biological resources and traditional knowledge utilized in the company's manufacturing, R&D, and product formulations.
  • • Identify products using Indian bio-resources and verify if the corresponding Form I filings have been completed with the NBA.
  • • Calculate and clear any outstanding ABS dues (ranging from 0.1% to 0.5% of ex-factory sales) to preempt potential enforcement actions, show-cause notices, or delays in patent approvals.
