NBA Enforces Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Compliance with INR 9.68 Cr Collection

Shift in Legal Position

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) reported a collection of INR 9.68 crore under the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework during April-September. This signals strict compliance monitoring under the Biological Diversity Act, contrasting with historical trends of low compliance and self-declaration backlogs by commercial users of biological resources.

Legal & In-House Action Items
  • Conduct an internal audit of all products and R&D pipelines utilizing Indian biological resources to verify if NBA approval for commercial utilization is required.
  • Review supply chains for botanical and biological materials to ensure compliance with the Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023.
  • Assess liability for Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) obligations and ensure timely payments to avoid prosecution and operational disruption.

Inauguration of New Agri-Biotech Facilities at ICAR-IIAB Ranchi

Shift in Legal Position

The government has inaugurated advanced research facilities at the Indian Institute of Agricultural Biotechnology (ICAR-IIAB) Ranchi, expanding state-supported R&D capabilities. This contrasts with the previous reliance of private agri-biotech firms on offshore or limited domestic private infrastructure.

Legal & In-House Action Items
  • Evaluate potential public-private research partnerships (PPPs) with ICAR-IIAB to leverage advanced diagnostic and genomic infrastructure.
  • Monitor technology transfer opportunities or patents arising from ICAR-IIAB's new facilities for commercial licensing.

National Review Workshop Focuses on Ground-Level Co-operative Action Plans

Shift in Legal Position

The 9th National Review Workshop on Co-operatives aims to convert the 'Sahkar se Samriddhi' vision into concrete grassroots models. This transitions the policy focus from high-level legislative changes to structural local-level cooperative integration, impacting entities engaging with rural credit and agri-cooperatives.

Legal & In-House Action Items
  • Assess business model linkages or procurement channels that interact with primary agricultural cooperative societies (PACS) to identify regional regulatory compliance requirements.
